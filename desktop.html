The order of the day
Formal Ceremony attire

Garden Formal

Dress up and bring the color! This is a garden party at golden hour, so think bright, bold, and joyful — the kind of outfit that makes you want to dance. We just ask that you leave white, ivory, and cream to the bride.

Ladies

Floor-length or tea-length gowns and dresses in vibrant colors and prints. Florals, ruffles, satin, and statement silhouettes are encouraged.

Gentlemen

Suits or tuxedos in lighter summer fabrics — linen, cotton, or lightweight wool. Color is welcome: think sky blue, cream, sage or emerald green, tan, or a fun pattern. Ties optional.

Shoes

The ceremony and reception are outdoors on garden paths and lawn — block heels, wedges, or flats are your friend.

Layers

Santa Barbara evenings get cool — bring a wrap, shawl, or light jacket.

Color

The brighter, the better. Have fun with it!
